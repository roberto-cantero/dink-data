# Build an accurate prototype first, and automate intake afterwards

On 20 October 2026 PickleCam's backend developer told us the priority is a working prototype, not a pipeline that collects videos as soon as they reach Bunny, and not cost optimization. Our first goal is therefore a prototype whose stats and reels are accurate enough to impress PickleCam's CEO, measured by the accuracy gates on held-out facilities. Until then we download about 150 games by hand from the facility dashboard, and `dink ingest` prepares each one. The read-only endpoint, `dink catalog sync`, automatic sampling, the daily fetch, delivery to players and the production cost work wait until after the prototype, and we build them around whatever PickleCam already runs. Choices that only lower production cost don't block prototype work, but we keep the contracts, timestamps and HMAC ids that make the later switch cheap.
