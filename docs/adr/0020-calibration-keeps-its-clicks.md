# Calibration keeps its clicks and fits each camera alone first

DESIGN 6.3 says the solver fits lens parameters jointly across cameras of the same model. We don't know which models PickleCam installed, so there is no key to group cameras by yet. The first solver therefore fits each camera alone, with weak priors on focal length, k1 and k2. It also has a joint fit that shares those three across a list of cameras and gives each camera a small correction. #50 decides which cameras form a lens group, once real residuals show whether single fits are good enough. Regrouping later means refitting, so the click tool saves the raw clicks next to each fit as `cameras/{camera_id}/v{n}/clicks.json`, a contract of its own. A refit, a solver fix or an edit then needs no new clicking, and it writes new versions of every camera it touches. A calibration's held-back error is the mean leave-one-out error over its named floor keypoints: each keypoint is refit without itself and measured. A fixed holdout would lose clicks on partial courts that have few of them, and the fit is cheap enough to run once per keypoint.
