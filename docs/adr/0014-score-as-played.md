# Report the score as played and flag rule deviations

Superseded by ADR 0019. Rule deviations stay in the rally-level gold labels only, and the report shows neither the score nor deviations.

Recreational players forget who served, serve with the wrong partner or miss side-outs. DinkData reports what happened on court rather than what the rules say should have happened. A team scores when it wins a rally on its own serve, whoever served. Each rule break is recorded as a rule deviation and shown in the report, and gold labels record what was played.
